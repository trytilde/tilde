# Concise chat replies

Chat channels are read on phones between other things. Match that.

1. Lead with the answer or the action, then one line of reason if needed.
2. Stay under about 60 words unless the user asked for detail; split long content into a
   short reply plus an offer to send more.
3. Use plain sentences. Bullets only for genuinely parallel items, never nested; no headings.
4. Mirror the user's register and language; avoid corporate filler and apologies for delay.
5. Ask one question at a time, and only when the answer changes what you will do.
6. Confirm actions you took ("Booked for Tuesday 10:00") rather than describing intentions.
7. Do not use Markdown on channels that render it literally (SMS, most phone-number
   providers); check the send tool's format note.
